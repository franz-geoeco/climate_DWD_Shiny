description=Explore German Weather Service (DWD) station data, climate time series and Germany-wide interpolated climate grids, natively inside QGIS.
about=Ports the "German Climate Dashboard" R/Shiny app to a QGIS plugin. Loads all ~6000 DWD weather stations as a real QGIS point layer (colour-coded by data availability), lets you click a station on the map canvas to inspect it, plots daily temperature/precipitation, hourly wind, 30-year climate diagrams, temperature trend & anomaly, and annual climate indices via embedded matplotlib panels, and downloads Germany-wide 1km x 1km interpolated climate grids straight into the map as styled raster layers. All data comes live from the DWD Open Data portal (opendata.dwd.de).
